Reducing Exposure Before Birth: An Interrupted Time Series Study of Prenatal Exposure to Fetotoxic Medications Under Risk Management Plans in Canada

Khan NS, Sheehy O, Freitas Leal L, Tchuente V, Bérard A.

Clinical Pharmacology & Therapeutics · 2026 · DOI: 10.1002/cpt.70353

Interrupted time series analysis of the Québec Pregnancy Cohort (~559,000 pregnancies, 1998–2022), evaluating whether Canadian risk management plans reduced first-trimester prenatal exposure to fetotoxic medications. Segmented quasi-Poisson regression with Newey-West HAC standard errors and Fourier seasonality adjustment.

Childhood and Adolescent Exposure to Chemicals Found in Personal Care Products

Institution

ESPUM, Université de Montréal

Programme

M.Sc., Occupational & Environmental Health — Research Option

Specialization

Environmental Epidemiology & Toxicology

Completed

2022 · GPA 4.00/4.30

Supervisor

Dr. Maryse Bouchard, Canada Research Chair on Environmental Contaminants and Populations' Health

Co-supervisor

Dr. Jillian Ashley-Martin (Health Canada; Washington University School of Medicine)

Scoping review of 37 biomonitoring studies on parabens, phenols, phthalates, and PFAS following personal care product use in children and adolescents aged 5–19. Found consistent associations between product types and specific chemical classes (makeup → parabens; lotion → phenols; perfume → phthalates) and highlighted gaps in methodological standardization across studies.
