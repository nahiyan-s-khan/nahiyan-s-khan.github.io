About

Researcher. Epidemiologist.
Future industry scientist.

PhD Candidate in Pharmaceutical Sciences at Université de Montréal, based at the Azrieli Research Centre, CHU Sainte-Justine, supervised by Prof. Anick Bérard (Canada Research Chair, Tier 1). My doctoral research evaluates the real-world impact of risk management plans on prenatal exposure to teratogenic and fetotoxic medications, using 25 years of data from the Quebec Pregnancy Cohort.
