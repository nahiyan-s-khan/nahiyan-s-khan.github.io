Fetal Exposure Ranking of Teratogenic Medications in Canada (TRIM-QPC)

2024–present
In progress — manuscript in preparation ★ First author

A replication of the US Teratogenic Risk Impact and Mitigation (TRIM) tool using administrative data from the Québec Pregnancy Cohort (2000–2020). Provides Canadian-specific fetal-exposure estimates for 61 drugs to inform RMP prioritization — a systematic tool that does not yet exist in Canada.

Regulatory Impact on Prenatal Clozapine Exposure: A Triangulated DiD and Synthetic Control Study

2024–present
In progress — analysis & manuscript

A cross-national difference-in-differences and synthetic control study evaluating whether the FDA’s 2015 Clozapine REMS reduced prenatal clozapine exposure. Uses US administrative pregnancy data (AM-PREGNANT) as the treated population and the Québec Pregnancy Cohort as the control (never subject to the REMS), 2004–2020.

Methodological contribution: triangulated quasi-experimental design with US/Canada comparative data, rare in perinatal pharmacoepidemiology.

Difference-in-Differences Synthetic Control REMS Causal Inference

Risk Management Programs for Fetotoxic Drugs Worldwide: A Narrative Review

2024–present
Under review — Drug Safety

A comprehensive comparative review of RMPs and Pregnancy Prevention Programs (PPPs) for isotretinoin, thalidomide/analogues (lenalidomide, pomalidomide), and valproate across 11 jurisdictions: USA, Canada, Brazil, Argentina, EU, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Saudi Arabia, Australia, and New Zealand.

Co-authored with Prof. Anick Bérard. Submitted to Drug Safety (Adis/Springer).

CAMCCO-L Case Study Narrative Reviews

2023–present
In progress — drafting

Narrative reviews converting structured CAMCCO-L case study presentations into publishable manuscripts. Each case study covers the pharmacology and teratogenicity of a drug class, its use during pregnancy, and the policy responses that followed.

Paroxetine & SSRIs / cardiac defects — Depression and anxiety in pregnancy, SSRI mechanism of action, postulated teratogenic pathways, role of pharmacoepidemiology and pharmacogenomics in disentangling drug-specific from class effects, and policy implications. Presented at CAMCCO-L, Barcelona (2023). Target: Frontiers for Pharmacoepidemiology.

Isotretinoin / acne & RMPs ★ First author — Acne burden, isotretinoin teratogenicity, RMP history (SMART, iPLEDGE, EU PPP), international comparisons, and post-Dobbs implications. Presented at CAMCCO-L, SSM Montréal (2025). Target: Frontiers for Pharmacoepidemiology.

Antiepileptic drugs / valproate — Valproate teratogenicity, neurodevelopmental outcomes, AED-exposed pregnancy epidemiology, and regulatory responses across jurisdictions.

Codeine use during breastfeeding — Pharmacokinetics of codeine and morphine, CYP2D6 pharmacogenomics, transfer into breast milk, and risk-benefit considerations for lactating persons. Based on CAMCCO-L Case Study 4.

Childhood & Adolescent Exposure to Chemicals in Personal Care Products

2022
✓ Completed — M.Sc. Thesis ★ First author

A scoping review synthesizing 37 biomonitoring studies on parabens, phenols, phthalates, and PFAS following personal care product use in children and adolescents aged 5–19. Conducted at the École de santé publique, Université de Montréal, supervised by Profs. Maryse Bouchard and Jillian Ashley-Martin.

Found consistent associations between product types and chemical classes (makeup → parabens; lotion → phenols; perfume → phthalates) and highlighted gaps in methodological standardization across studies.

Impact of Canadian Risk Management Plans on Prenatal Exposure to Fetotoxic Medications

2026
✓ Published — Clin Pharmacol Ther ★ First author

A population-based interrupted time series (ITS) analysis using the Québec Pregnancy Cohort (QPC, 1998–2022) evaluating whether Canadian RMPs reduced first-trimester prenatal exposure to teratogenic and fetotoxic medications. Quasi-Poisson segmented regression with Fourier seasonality terms; subgroup analyses for retinoids and valproic acid.

First-author study, supervised by Prof. Anick Bérard, in collaboration with biostatistician Odile Sheehy. Presented as a poster at ISPE 2026, Milan.

Khan et al. 2026 · doi:10.1002/cpt.70353
